These terms explain how bookings, deposits, payments and cancellations work when you book a safari, Kilimanjaro climb, Zanzibar holiday, day trip or other travel service with Serengeti Roaming African Safaris. By confirming a booking, you agree to them.

## Booking and confirmation

- Your booking is confirmed once we have received your deposit and sent you a written confirmation by email or WhatsApp.
- The lead traveller who makes the booking is responsible for giving correct details (full names as in passports, dates of birth, dietary and medical needs) for everyone on the booking.
- All bookings depend on availability of accommodation, park permits, flights and guides at the time of confirmation.

## Prices and what's included

- Prices are in US dollars unless we tell you otherwise.
- What is included is listed in your itinerary and quote — usually accommodation, meals as stated, park and conservation fees, transport in a private safari vehicle and a professional guide.
- Unless your itinerary says otherwise, international flights, visas, travel insurance, drinks, tips and personal expenses are not included.
- Prices can change before you pay in full if government taxes, park fees or supplier rates change. Once you have paid in full, your price is fixed.

## Payments and deposits

- A 30% deposit is required to confirm your booking.
- The balance is due 60 days before your trip starts.
- If you book within 60 days of your trip, the full amount is due when you book.
- You can pay by bank transfer or card; the details are on your quote or invoice. Bank and card fees are paid by the traveller.
- If the balance is not paid on time, we may cancel the booking and the cancellation charges below apply.

## Cancellations and refunds

Please send cancellations in writing by email. Charges depend on how close to your arrival you cancel:

- 60 days or more before arrival: we keep the deposit.
- 30–59 days before arrival: 50% of the total trip price.
- 15–29 days before arrival: 75% of the total trip price.
- 14 days or less, or no-show: 100% of the total trip price.

There are no refunds for unused services once your trip has started. If we have to cancel your trip ourselves, you can choose a full refund or new dates.

## Changes and amendments

- We will try to make any changes you ask for after confirmation, but they depend on availability and may cost more.
- We may need to make small changes — for example a similar lodge or a different route — for reasons outside our control. We will tell you about any important change as early as possible.

## Travel insurance

Travel insurance is required for every trip. It should cover medical treatment, emergency evacuation, cancellation and personal liability. For Kilimanjaro climbs it must cover trekking up to 6,000 m. We may ask to see your policy before travel.

## Passports, visas and health

- You are responsible for your passport, visas and vaccinations for Tanzania and any other country on your trip.
- Your passport should be valid for at least six months after you arrive and have blank pages.
- A yellow fever certificate may be needed if you arrive from, or travel through, a country with a risk of yellow fever. Ask your doctor about other vaccinations and malaria prevention.

## Health and fitness

You must be fit enough for the activities you book. Please tell us about any medical conditions, allergies, dietary needs or mobility limitations when you book, so we can plan safely. Climbing Kilimanjaro is demanding at high altitude; please see your doctor before you book.

## Liability

We work with carefully chosen lodges, drivers, guides and activity providers. Some services on your trip are provided by these independent suppliers. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for loss, injury, damage, delay or extra costs caused by them, by your own actions, or by events outside our control.

## Wildlife and adventure risks

Safaris, climbs and water activities involve natural risks. Wild animals are unpredictable, so always follow your guide's instructions, stay in the vehicle unless your guide says otherwise, and never feed or approach animals.

## Behaviour and conduct

Please respect local laws and customs, park rules, your guides and other travellers. We or our suppliers may end the services of anyone whose behaviour puts others at risk, without a refund.

## Complaints

If something is not right during your trip, please tell your guide or contact us straight away so we can try to fix it. If you are still not happy, send us your complaint in writing within 30 days of the end of your trip.

## Photos and reviews

We may ask to use photos or reviews you share with us on our website or social media. We will only use them with your permission, and you can ask us to remove them at any time.

## Questions

If you have any questions about these terms, contact us before you book — we are happy to help.
