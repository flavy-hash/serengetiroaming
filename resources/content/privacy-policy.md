Serengeti Roaming African Safaris ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or plan a trip with us, why, and for how long.

## What we collect

When you contact us, ask a question, request a quote or book a trip, we collect the information you choose to share so we can reply and arrange your travel:

- your name and contact details (email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country);
- trip details (travel dates, number of travellers, preferences, budget and questions);
- anything else you write in a message, review or booking request.

## How we use your information

We use your information only to:

- answer your messages and give travel advice;
- prepare quotes and itineraries, and manage your bookings;
- send confirmations, updates and important information about your trip;
- publish a review you send us, if you asked us to and only with the name you gave.

Instant quotes are emailed to our office so we can follow up with you. The download link we give you stops working after 7 days.

## Sharing with third parties

We share only what is needed to arrange your trip — for example with lodges and camps, transport providers, guides, park authorities or activity operators. We never sell your personal information.

## Anonymous visitor statistics

To understand which pages and safaris are popular, we count page views without cookies and without storing your IP address. For each page view we keep:

- the page you looked at (and which safari, if any);
- the type of device (mobile, tablet or desktop);
- the website that sent you here, if any (for example "google.com");
- your country, worked out from your IP address at the moment of the visit — the IP address itself is never saved;
- a random-looking visitor ID that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you.

We do not count visits when your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control". These statistics are deleted automatically after 180 days. Country lookups use [IP geolocation by DB-IP](https://db-ip.com).

## Maps and translations

Route maps on safari pages load map pictures from OpenStreetMap (or Mapbox) only when you scroll to the map. If you choose another language with the language button, the page is translated by Google Translate.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit who can see it. No website or email system is completely secure, but we work to keep the risk low.

## Retention

We keep your details only as long as we need them to answer you, run your trip, meet legal and accounting requirements, or keep proper business records.

## External links

This website links to other sites, such as our social media pages and review sites. We are not responsible for how they handle your data — please read their privacy policies.

## Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details you sent us. Use the contact details below.
